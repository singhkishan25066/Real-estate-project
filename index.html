The 5 Pillars of Due Diligence

We reject speculative promises in favor of absolute legal and cadastral sovereignty. Every asset syndicated under Township Capital meets an unbending 5-point verification framework prior to client allocation.

01 / PILLAR

RERA Statutory Registration

Every layout and multi-story blueprint strictly adheres to Real Estate Regulatory Authority mandates, ensuring public escrow protection and sanctioned site timelines.

Document: Approved Layout Order
02 / PILLAR

Physical Demarcation

No paper-only claims. Every plot is cast with reinforced concrete survey cornerstones, visible cadastral markers, and wide graded right-of-way roads before registry.

Document: Cadastral Field GPS Map
03 / PILLAR

Khata & Khesra Audit

Complete 30-year revenue ledger tracing across local Anchal offices, ensuring clean mutation (Dakhil-Kharij), current rent receipts, and clear Jamabandi numbers.

Document: 30-Yr Search Report

Non-Encumbrance Guarantee

Formal non-encumbrance certificate (Form 16) issued by the Sub-Registrar confirming unencumbered title free from liens, pending court litigation, or state ceiling claims.

Document: Nil-Encumbrance Certificate
05 / PILLAR

Master Road Width Standards

No cramped interior alleys. Strict dedication of 25ft to 35ft arterial right-of-way guarantees unrestricted heavy vehicle transit and substantial long-term appreciation.

Document: Master Road Deed Dedication
